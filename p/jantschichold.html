扬·奇肖尔德 Jan Tschichold

Penguin Books

Die Neue Typographie · Penguin 重新设计师

graphictypography
1902 生于莱比锡，1974 去世。1923 接触包豪斯。1928 著作《Die Neue Typographie》——现代主义不对称字体设计宣言。1933 逃离纳粹德国。1947 受聘于企鹅图书，完全重设全部企鹅书系标准化版面（沿用至今）。1967 设计 Sabon 字体。从激进现代主义到古典字体的晚期转向是设计史著名转折。
Born 1902 Leipzig, died 1974. Bauhaus exposure 1923. Authored 'Die Neue Typographie' (1928) — the manifesto of modernist asymmetric type design. Fled Nazi Germany 1933. Hired by Penguin Books 1947 — completely redesigned all Penguin titles into the iconic standardized format used today. Designed Sabon typeface (1967). His shift from radical modernism to classical typography late in career remains a famous design trajectory.
