深泽直人 Naoto Fukasawa

MUJI / ±0

MUJI 设计顾问 · ±0 创始人

industrial
1956 年生于日本山梨县。多摩美术大学毕业。先后加入 IDEO 日本，2003 年自创深泽直人设计事务所。自 2002 起担任 MUJI 顾问董事会成员（同期还有原研哉、Sam Hecht、Jasper Morrison）。设计 MUJI 壁挂式 CD 播放器（1999）、MUJI 加湿器、±0 品牌加湿器。与 Jasper Morrison 共同提出「Super Normal」哲学。Without Thought 工作坊方法论。英国皇家工业设计师。21_21 Design Sight 馆长。
Born 1956 in Yamanashi. Tama Art University. Joined IDEO Japan, then founded Naoto Fukasawa Design 2003. MUJI Advisory Board member since 2002 (alongside Kenya Hara, Sam Hecht, Jasper Morrison). Designed MUJI wall-mounted CD player (1999), MUJI humidifier, ±0 brand humidifier. Coined 'super normal' philosophy with Jasper Morrison. Without Thought workshop methodology. Royal Designer for Industry (UK). Currently Director of 21_21 Design Sight (Tokyo).
