山姆·赫克特 Sam Hecht

Industrial Facility

Industrial Facility 联创 · MUJI 设计核心

industrial
1969 生于伦敦。皇家艺术学院。1991-2002 在 IDEO（旧金山 → 东京）。2002 与 Kim Colin 在伦敦联创 Industrial Facility。2003 起 MUJI 顾问董事会成员。设计 MUJI 厨房秤、MUJI 计算器、Herman Miller、Mattiazzi、Established & Sons。安静、深思、超普通的美学风格。
Born 1969 London. Royal College of Art. Worked at IDEO 1991-2002 (San Francisco then Tokyo). Co-founded Industrial Facility 2002 in London with Kim Colin. MUJI Advisory Board since 2003 alongside Naoto Fukasawa. Designed MUJI products (kitchen scales, calculators), Herman Miller, Mattiazzi, Established & Sons. Quiet, considered, super-normal aesthetic.
