柳原照弘 Teruhiro Yanagihara

Teruhiro Yanagihara Studio

工业设计师 / 艺术总监

industrial
1976 年生于日本香川县。1999 年毕业于大阪艺术大学，2002 年在大阪创立工作室，现以神户与法国阿尔勒为据点。其跨学科实践涵盖产品、室内、纺织与品牌艺术指导。受聘为 Karimoku New Standard 创意总监，为这家传统日本家具厂牌统筹系列。客户包括 Kvadrat、1616/Arita Japan、Offecct，作品被阿姆斯特丹市立博物馆与巴黎国立美术中心永久收藏。
Born 1976 in Kagawa,Japan. Graduated from Osaka University of Arts in 1999 and established his studio in Osaka in 2002,now based in Kobe and Arles,France. His interdisciplinary practice spans products,interiors,textiles and brand art direction. Appointed creative director of Karimoku New Standard,art-directing collections for the traditional Japanese furniture maker. Works for Kvadrat,1616/Arita Japan and Offecct,in the permanent collections of the Stedelijk Museum and Paris's national fine-arts centre.
