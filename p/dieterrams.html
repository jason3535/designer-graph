迪特·拉姆斯 Dieter Rams

Braun

Braun 设计总监（1961-1995）· 好设计十大原则

industrial
1932 年生于德国威斯巴登。1955 加入博朗，1961-1995 担任设计总监，定义了博朗简洁功能性美学。代表作：SK 4 留声机（1956）、606 通用书架系统（1960）、T3 便携收音机（1958，直接启发 iPod）。70 年代提出「好设计十大原则」。「Less, but better」（少而精）哲学深刻影响乔尼·艾维和苹果。德意志联邦共和国功勋十字勋章持有者。Vitsoe 至今仍生产他的家具。
Born 1932 in Wiesbaden, Germany. Joined Braun in 1955, Chief of Design 1961-1995. Defined Braun's clean functional aesthetic. Iconic products: SK 4 record player (1956), 606 Universal Shelving System (1960), T3 pocket radio (1958, direct inspiration for the iPod). Authored '10 Principles for Good Design' (1970s). Philosophy 'Less, but better' (Weniger, aber besser) became foundational influence on Jony Ive at Apple. Officer of the Order of Merit Federal Republic of Germany. Vitsoe still produces his furniture today.
