2021 年起任 Stripe 设计负责人,此前分别主管 Lyft 体验设计与 Airbnb 设计——连续三家定义了 2010-20 年代设计范式的公司。她提出「MVQP」(最小可行质量产品)来回应 MVP:效用 → 易用 → 美感的三级标准,主张「工艺」不是最后再加的一层奢侈品,而是软件之所以值得信任的原因。《快公司》商业界百位最具创造力人物之一。
Head of design at Stripe since 2021, previously head of experience design at Lyft and head of design at Airbnb — three consecutive definitive design orgs of the 2010s-20s. She is known for the \"MVQP\" idea (minimum viable quality product) as a rebuttal to MVP: a three-tier bar of Utility → Usability → Beauty, arguing that craft is not a luxury layer added at the end but the thing that makes software trustworthy. One of Fast Company's 100 Most Creative People in Business.
