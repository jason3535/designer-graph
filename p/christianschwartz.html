1977 年生于美国新罕布什尔州康科德。毕业于卡内基梅隆大学传达设计专业。先在柏林 MetaDesign 为大众汽车开发字体，后加入 Font Bureau（与 Tobias Frere-Jones 共事），2001 年独立执业。2007 年与伦敦设计师 Paul Barnes 联合创办 Commercial Type。代表作 Neutraface（2002）、Graphik（2009）及权威的 Neue Haas Grotesk 复刻（2010）。曾与 Erik Spiekermann 合作 Bosch、德国铁路字体。
Born 1977 in Concord,New Hampshire. Graduated from Carnegie Mellon's Communication Design program. First worked at MetaDesign Berlin developing typefaces for Volkswagen,then joined Font Bureau alongside Tobias Frere-Jones before going independent in 2001. Co-founded Commercial Type with London designer Paul Barnes in 2007. Designed Neutraface (2002),Graphik (2009) and the definitive Neue Haas Grotesk revival (2010). Collaborated with Erik Spiekermann on Bosch and Deutsche Bahn type.
