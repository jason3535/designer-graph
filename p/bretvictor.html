布雷特·维克托 Bret Victor

Dynamicland

交互设计哲学家 · Inventing on Principle

ux
约 1976 生。加州理工本，UC 伯克利硕士。苹果人机界面设计师（参与 iPad 概念）。2014 创立 Dynamicland（奥克兰研究实验室）。著名 2012 演讲「Inventing on Principle」——定义了实时反馈交互系统的愿景。后续文章《Up and Down the Ladder of Abstraction》《Magic Ink》《Drawing Dynamic Visualizations》成为 HCI 圈经典。极少出现于公众，但在交互设计圈影响力巨大。
Born ~1976. Caltech BS, UC Berkeley MS. Apple Human Interface Designer (worked on iPad concept). Founded Dynamicland 2014 (research lab in Oakland). Famous 2012 talk 'Inventing on Principle' — defining vision for live-coding-feedback interactive systems. Subsequent essays 'Up and Down the Ladder of Abstraction', 'Magic Ink', 'Drawing Dynamic Visualizations' became seminal HCI references. Reclusive, highly influential among interaction designers.
