1929 生于纽约，2020 去世。Cooper Union、博洛尼亚 Academia di Belle Arti。1954 联创 Pushpin Studios。1974 创办 Milton Glaser Inc。代表作：I ♥ NY logo（1977，免费为纽约州设计，成为史上最被模仿的 logo）、Bob Dylan 海报（1966）、DC Comics logo(1977)、布鲁克林啤酒厂 logo(1996)。2009 国家艺术奖章——首位获此奖的平面设计师。SVA 教授 50+ 年。AIGA 奖章 1972。
Born 1929 NYC, died 2020. Cooper Union, Academia di Belle Arti Bologna. Co-founded Pushpin Studios 1954. Founded Milton Glaser Inc 1974. Designed: I ♥ NY logo (1977, pro bono for NY State, became most-imitated logo ever), Bob Dylan poster (1966), DC Comics logo (1977), Brooklyn Brewery logo (1996). National Medal of Arts 2009 — first graphic designer to receive it. SVA professor 50+ years. AIGA Medal 1972.
