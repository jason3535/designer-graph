原研哉 Kenya Hara

MUJI Art Director

MUJI 艺术总监 · 「白」哲学

graphicindustrial
1958 生于日本冈山。武藏野美术大学。Hara Design Institute 总裁（隶属日本设计中心）。2002 起任 MUJI 艺术总监——定义 MUJI 视觉识别（那些极简「地平线 horizon」广告）。设计 Tsutaya、六本木 Hills 识别。著作《Designing Design》(2007)、《白》(2010)、《Ex-formation》(2017)。开创将「emptiness 留白」作为日本设计哲学输出全球。
Born 1958 Okayama, Japan. Musashino Art University. Hara Design Institute President (Nippon Design Center). MUJI Art Director since 2002 — defines MUJI's visual identity (those minimalist 地平线/horizon ad campaigns). Designed identity for Tsutaya, Roppongi Hills. Authored 'Designing Design' (2007), 'White' (2010), 'Ex-formation' (2017). Pioneered 'emptiness' as Japanese design philosophy worth exporting globally.
