查尔斯·伊姆斯（1907-1978）和蕾·凯瑟·伊姆斯（1912-1988）夫妇 1941 在洛杉矶创办伊姆斯工作室。开创模压胶合板家具（LCW 椅 1946）、玻璃纤维壳椅（DSW 1950）、伊姆斯休闲椅（1956）。家具之外：Case Study House #8 自宅、《Powers of Ten》(1977)电影、IBM 展览。20 世纪最具影响力的美国设计师组合。Herman Miller 和 Vitra 至今出品其作品。
Charles Eames (1907-1978) and Ray Kaiser Eames (1912-1988). Husband-and-wife duo founded Eames Office 1941 in Los Angeles. Pioneered molded plywood furniture (LCW chair 1946), fiberglass shells (DSW chair 1950), the Eames Lounge Chair (1956). Beyond furniture: Case Study House #8, films like 'Powers of Ten' (1977), exhibitions for IBM. The most influential American design partnership of the 20th century. Their work continues at Herman Miller and Vitra.
