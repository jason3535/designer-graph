克里斯·索尔斯比 Kris Sowersby

Klim Type Foundry

Klim 字体铸造厂创始人

typographygraphic
1981 年生于新西兰，主要为自学成才。曾就读旺加努伊设计学院平面设计专业。2005 年在惠灵顿创立 Klim Type Foundry。其精雕细琢的字体深植于字体历史：Feijoa 与 National（2007，纽约 TDC 卓越证书）、Founders Grotesk（2009）、Tiempos（2010）及 Domaine。苹果为其设备授权了 Founders Grotesk 与 Domaine Display。曾与 Erik Spiekermann、Christian Schwartz 合作设计 FF Meta Serif 与 FF Unit Slab。2013 年 AGI 会员。
Born 1981 in New Zealand,largely self-taught after studying graphic design at Wanganui School of Design. Founded Klim Type Foundry in Wellington in 2005. His meticulously crafted typefaces engage deeply with typographic history: Feijoa and National (2007,TDC Certificate of Excellence),Founders Grotesk (2009),Tiempos (2010) and Domaine. Apple licensed Founders Grotesk and Domaine Display for its devices. Collaborated with Erik Spiekermann and Christian Schwartz on FF Meta Serif and FF Unit Slab. AGI member 2013.
