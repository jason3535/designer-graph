1914 生于瑞士拉珀斯维尔，1996 去世。瑞士国际主义字体风格的定义性人物——网格系统、无衬线字体、不对称版面、摄影优先于插画。设计 50/60 年代苏黎世 Tonhalle 音乐厅海报。著作《Grid Systems in Graphic Design》(1981)成为网格设计圣经。1958 创办 Neue Grafik 杂志。
Born 1914 Rapperswil Switzerland, died 1996. Defining figure of Swiss International Typographic Style — grid systems, sans-serif type, asymmetric layouts, photography-over-illustration. Designed iconic Zurich Tonhalle concert posters (1950s-60s). Authored 'Grid Systems in Graphic Design' (1981) — became the bible of grid-based design. Founded Neue Grafik magazine 1958. Co-founded the magazine that defined the Swiss Style abroad.
