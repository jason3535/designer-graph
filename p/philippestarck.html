菲利普·斯塔克 Philippe Starck

Starck Network

法国跨界设计师 · 万物皆可设计

industrial
1949 生于巴黎。Camondo 艺术学院。开始任 Pierre Cardin 艺术总监（1968）。1984 设计 Café Costes 引爆事业。代表作：Juicy Salif 柠檬榨汁器（Alessi 1990）、Louis Ghost 椅（Kartell 2002）。设计涵盖家具、酒店、游艇（包括为乔布斯设计的 Venus 号）、手表、牙刷。20 世纪末最多产的商业设计师。法国荣誉军团勋章多次获颁。
Born 1949 Paris. École Camondo. Began as Pierre Cardin Art Director (1968). Designed Café Costes 1984 (career-defining). Iconic: Juicy Salif lemon squeezer (Alessi 1990), Louis Ghost chair (Kartell 2002), Mickey Mouse-themed Mademoiselle chair. Designs span furniture, hotels, yachts (including for Steve Jobs), watches, toothbrushes. Most prolific commercial designer of late 20th century. Multiple French Légion d'honneur.
