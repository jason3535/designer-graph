埃里克·施皮克曼 Erik Spiekermann

MetaDesign / FontShop

FontShop 创始人 · FF Meta/汉莎字体

graphic
1947 生于德国 Stadthagen。柏林自由大学（艺术史）。1979 联创 MetaDesign（柏林，德国最大设计公司之一）。1989 联创 FontShop——全球第一家数字字体零售商。设计 FF Meta 字体（1991）——被誉为「90 年代的 Helvetica」。设计奥迪、大众、汉莎、德国铁路企业字体。AIGA 奖章 2011。英国名誉皇家工业设计师。著作《Stop Stealing Sheep》。
Born 1947 Stadthagen, Germany. Free University Berlin (art history). Co-founded MetaDesign 1979 (Berlin, became one of largest design firms). Co-founded FontShop 1989 (first digital type retailer). Designed FF Meta typeface (1991) — called 'the Helvetica of the 90s'. Designed identity for Audi, Volkswagen, Lufthansa, Deutsche Bahn corporate type. AIGA Medal 2011. Honorary Royal Designer for Industry. Author 'Stop Stealing Sheep' on typography.
