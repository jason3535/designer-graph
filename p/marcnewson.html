马克·纽森 Marc Newson

LoveFrom / Apple

Apple Watch 联设 · LoveFrom 联创

industrial
1963 生于悉尼。悉尼艺术学院。1986 设计的 Lockheed Lounge 椅 2009 拍出 240 万英镑（在世设计师最高拍卖价）。客户：Cappellini、Apple、Ford、Nike、澳航（机舱内饰）。2012 获 CBE 爵位。2014 加入苹果任特别项目设计师，与艾维合作主导 Apple Watch 设计。2019 与艾维联创 LoveFrom。《时代》2005 年百大最具影响力人物。
Born 1963 Sydney. Sydney College of the Arts. Iconic 1986 Lockheed Lounge sold for £2.4M (highest price for living designer). Designed for Cappellini, Apple, Ford, Nike, Qantas (interior). CBE 2012. Joined Apple as Special Projects Designer 2014, co-led Apple Watch design with Jony Ive. Co-founded LoveFrom 2019 with Ive. TIME 100 most influential 2005.
