大卫·卡森 David Carson

Ray Gun magazine

反网格设计 · Ray Gun 主美

graphic
1955 生于德州 Corpus Christi。自学（前职业冲浪运动员）。1992-1995 任 Ray Gun 杂志艺术总监——创造 90 年代最具影响力的反网格、解构主义编辑设计。客户：百事、耐克、MTV、索尼、微软。著作《The End of Print》(1995)售出 20 万+ 册。启发了一代 grunge / 实验设计师。反穆勒-布罗克曼哲学。
Born 1955 Corpus Christi Texas. Self-taught (was a pro surfer). Art Director at Ray Gun magazine 1992-1995 — created the most influential anti-grid, deconstructed editorial design of the 90s. Designed for Pepsi, Nike, MTV, Sony, Microsoft. 'The End of Print' (1995) sold 200K+ copies. Inspired a generation of grunge / experimental designers. Anti-Müller-Brockmann philosophy.
