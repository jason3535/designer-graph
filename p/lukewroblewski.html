卢克·罗布莱夫斯基 Luke Wroblewski

Google (formerly)

数字产品领袖 ·《Mobile First》作者

ux
国际公认的数字产品领袖，其作品触达逾 7 亿用户。获伊利诺伊大学艺术史学士与平面设计美术学士。曾任 NCSA 高级界面设计师、eBay 首席 UI 设计师、雅虎首席设计架构师（副总裁）。联合创办 Bagcheck（2011 被 Twitter 收购）与 Polar（2014 被 Google 收购），在 Google 主导跨产品的产品、UX 与研究工作。在 2011 年同名著作中提出影响深远的「Mobile First」原则。
Internationally recognized digital product leader whose work has reached over 700 million people. Holds a BA in Art History and BFA in Graphic Design from the University of Illinois. Was Senior Interface Designer at NCSA,Lead UI Designer at eBay,and Chief Design Architect (VP) at Yahoo. Co-founded Bagcheck (acquired by Twitter,2011) and Polar (acquired by Google,2014),where he led product,UX and research for Google's cross-product efforts. Coined the influential 'Mobile First' principle in his 2011 book.
