莫莉·安德森 Molly Anderson

Apple

Apple 工业设计副总裁（后乔尼·艾维时代）

industrial
苹果现任工业设计负责人。乔尼·艾维 2019 年离开后，继任者埃文斯·汉基（Evans Hankey）离职留下领导真空，安德森随后升任工业设计副总裁。自 2016 年起与苹果工业设计专利相关联，参与了 Apple Pencil Pro 及近期 MacBook 的设计。与前任不同，她的话语权不再「一人独大」，反映出苹果在后艾维时代从「明星设计总监」转向更扁平、平台驱动的结构。2026 年 6 月，她作为评委出现在苹果重启的 Design Miami.Paris「Designers of Tomorrow」计划中，标志苹果对外叙事转向扶持新生代设计师。
Apple's current head of industrial design, appointed VP after Evans Hankey's departure left a leadership gap following Jony Ive's 2019 exit. Associated with Apple ID patents since 2016 and credited on the Apple Pencil Pro and recent MacBook work. Unlike her predecessors she holds less singular authority, reflecting Apple's post-Ive shift from a 'star design director' model to a flatter, platform-driven structure. Surfaced publicly in June 2026 as a juror for Apple's revived 'Designers of Tomorrow' program at Design Miami.Paris, signaling a pivot toward empowering a younger generation of designers.
