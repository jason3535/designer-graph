谷歌搜索用户体验副总裁VP of UX, Google Search

ux
在产品设计领域工作 20 多年，起步于 BBC 早期的数字化业务。此后在 Zynga 负责设计，经历其向移动优先社交游戏的转型，其间推出了《Words With Friends》；又在 NerdWallet 搭建并扩充 UX 团队，随后加入谷歌，先负责搜索广告的设计。现任谷歌搜索用户体验副总裁，带领设计师、研究员和内容策略团队负责全部搜索体验，包括 AI 概览（AI Overviews）与 AI Mode（2025 年 3 月在 Labs 试验，5 月向全美用户开放）。她强调要提升搜索的工艺水准，同时把搜索从静态结果页推向流动的 AI 生成回答；Fast Company 将谷歌评为 2026 年度设计公司时，AI Mode 是被点名的作品之一。
Has spent more than 20 years in product design, starting in the BBC's early digital efforts. She then led design at Zynga during its shift to mobile-first social games, a stretch that included Words With Friends, and built and scaled the UX team at NerdWallet before joining Google, where she first led design for Search Ads. As VP of UX for Google Search she leads the designers, researchers and content strategists behind all Search experiences, including AI Overviews and AI Mode (Labs experiment March 2025, rolled out to all US users May 2025). Her stated focus is raising the level of craftsmanship in Search while moving it from a static results page toward fluid, AI-generated answers; AI Mode was among the work cited when Fast Company named Google its 2026 Design Company of the Year.
