黎巴嫩字体设计师，当代阿拉伯文字体领域的领军人物。先后获贝鲁特美国大学学士、雷丁大学字体设计硕士、莱顿大学阿拉伯文易读性博士（2012）。任 Linotype/Monotype 阿拉伯文专家期间（2005-2018）设计了 Helvetica、Univers、Zapfino 的阿拉伯文配套版本。最著名的是 Frutiger Arabic（2007）——作为 Adrian Frutiger 人文无衬线体的官方阿拉伯文配套，由其本人直接参与开发。现于伦敦经营 ArabicType。
Lebanese type designer,a leading figure in contemporary Arabic typography. Earned a BA from the American University of Beirut,an MA in typeface design from the University of Reading,and a PhD on Arabic legibility from Leiden University (2012). As Arabic specialist at Linotype/Monotype (2005-2018) she designed the Arabic companions to Helvetica,Univers and Zapfino. Most notably she created Frutiger Arabic (2007) as an official companion to Adrian Frutiger's humanist sans,developed with his direct input. Runs ArabicType in London.
