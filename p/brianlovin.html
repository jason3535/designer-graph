旧金山的设计师与软件工程师，现于 Notion 设计 AI 产品。职业起步为 Buffer 首位产品设计师，后在 Facebook、Messenger、WhatsApp 与 Instagram 设计支付体验。联合创办品牌社区平台 Spectrum，被 GitHub 收购——他在 GitHub 用四年设计 GitHub Mobile 应用。随后联合创办团队协作工具 Campsite，被 Notion 收购。曾主持 Design Details 播客九年，并创办 Staff Design 访谈项目。
Designer and software engineer in San Francisco,now designing AI products at Notion. Cut his teeth as the first product designer at Buffer,then designed payments experiences across Facebook,Messenger,WhatsApp and Instagram. Co-founded Spectrum,acquired by GitHub — where he spent four years designing the GitHub Mobile apps. He then co-founded Campsite,a team-collaboration app acquired by Notion. Co-hosted the Design Details podcast for nine years and created the Staff Design interview project.
