马西莫·维涅利 Massimo Vignelli

Vignelli Associates

纽约地铁地图 · Helvetica 倡导者

graphic
1931 生于米兰，2014 去世于纽约。米兰理工大学。1965 联创 Unimark International（后 1971 创办 Vignelli Associates）。设计：纽约地铁标识系统（1970，与 Bob Noorda 合作，沿用 50+ 年）、美国航空识别（1968-2013）、Bloomingdale's 棕色购物袋、Knoll logo、纽约 Saint Peter 教堂。Helvetica 字体的强烈倡导者。「If you can design one thing, you can design everything」。AIGA 奖章 1983。
Born 1931 Milan, died 2014 NYC. Polytechnic University of Milan. Co-founded Unimark International 1965 (later Vignelli Associates 1971). Designed: NYC Subway signage system (1970, with Bob Noorda, used for 50+ years), American Airlines identity (1968-2013), Bloomingdale's brown bag, Knoll logo, Saint Peter's Church NYC. Strong advocate for Helvetica typeface. 'If you can design one thing, you can design everything.' AIGA Medal 1983.
