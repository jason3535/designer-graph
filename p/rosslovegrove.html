罗斯·拉夫格罗夫 Ross Lovegrove

Lovegrove Studio

英国设计 · 'Organic Essentialism'

industrial
1958 生于英国卡迪夫。皇家艺术学院。1980 年代加入 frog design（Sony Walkman、苹果项目）。1990 在伦敦创立 Lovegrove Studio。提出「Organic Essentialism」（有机本质主义）——数字制造支持的仿生形态。设计 Sony Walkman、Olympus Eye Trek 眼镜、Ty Nant 矿泉水瓶、Bombay Sapphire 杜松子酒瓶、Air One 椅（Edra）。英国皇家工业设计师。
Born 1958 Cardiff Wales. Royal College of Art. Joined frog design 1980s (worked on Sony Walkman, Apple). Founded Lovegrove Studio 1990 London. Coined 'Organic Essentialism' — bio-inspired forms enabled by digital fabrication. Designed Sony Walkman, Olympus Eye Trek glasses, Ty Nant water bottle, Bombay Sapphire bottle, Air One chair (Edra). Royal Designer for Industry.
